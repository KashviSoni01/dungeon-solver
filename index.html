◆ WHAT AWAITS INSIDE ◆

Forge Your Path to the Treasure

Procedural Labyrinths

Every dungeon is freshly carved with DFS maze generation, guaranteed solvable, with varied loops and hidden corridors.

Keys & Locked Doors

Find the golden key before reaching the treasure. Paths beyond the locked door are unreachable until you hold it.

Hidden Traps

Deadly spikes lie off the safe path. Step on one, and you return to the dungeon entrance to try again.

Moving Guardians

Silent treasure guardians patrol set routes on Levels II and III. Predict their path, or be caught and reset.

Increasing Difficulty

Grow from a 15×15 beginner catacomb with 2 traps, to a 21×21 ancient vault with 6 traps and two moving guardians.

Strategic Movement

Every move counts. Minimize your step count, race the timer, and master each labyrinth for the best completion time.

◆ SURVIVAL GUIDE ◆

How to Conquer the Dungeon

Five simple rules. One chance. Learn them well.

01

A S D

Master the Controls

Move with WASD or the Arrow Keys. Use Esc to pause, R to restart, and L to open Level Select.

02

Find the Golden Key

Deep inside the maze waits an ancient key. Without it, the treasure door will never open — and you'll need to go back.

03

Pass Through the Locked Door

The sealed door blocks the direct path. Once you carry the key, it swings aside — and the treasure finally lies within reach.

04

Avoid Traps & Guardians

Hidden spike fields reset your run. Patrolling guardians catch you on sight. Study their routes and time every crossing.

05

Reach the Treasure

The glowing chest waits in the deepest chamber. Touch it to seal your victory — then descend into the next, darker trial.
